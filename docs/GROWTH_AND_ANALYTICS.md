# Kairo Knox — discovery, community and measurement playbook

Adopted: 2026-10-25. This is an **experiment plan**, not a promise of virality or a verified causal conclusion.

## Objective and channels
**Kairo Knox** is a recognizable, evidence-first AI and cybersecurity investigator. Combine compelling source-checked stories, genuine demonstrations, and the creator's gradually emerging perspective. Preserve the approved black masked persona and creator privacy. Threads is the conversation hub; TikTok and YouTube Shorts are visual discovery; Instagram/Facebook can be added after separate creator-approved connections and release permissions. Use per-platform copy.

## Content format
- Hook in the first 1–3 seconds with an accurate, useful claim or challenge.
- Give immediate evidence: sourced finding or genuine screen-recorded test.
- Explain the limitation / counterargument and concrete implication.
- End with one relevant question or testable lesson; avoid generic "follow for more".
- Maintain 2 regular episodes : 1 interactive Micro Lab and consistent Fraser / original Kairo identity.
- For creator footage, choose opt-in faceless desk view, real screen capture or creator narration. Remove identifying information, license keys, user data and sensitive infrastructure.

## Creator reveal phases
1. **Investigator**: "I examined..." only if personally examined; explicitly distinguish third-party reported results.
2. **Builder**: screen-capture legitimate experiments, setbacks, meaningful outcomes, and what changed after testing.
3. **Optional creator acknowledgement**: creator speaks as Kairo's investigator or gives a first-person introduction, but retains mask, mystery and choice of privacy. No compulsory appearance disclosure.

## Measurement protocol
Measure **7 days and 30 days** consistently on the same weekday; inspect performance 24h, 72h, and 7 days after each post. Treat cross-platform counts as separate definitions (Threads viewers vs TikTok views vs unique viewers). Do not confuse a personally owned Threads account's other entertainment posts with Kairo-specific content. Classify each post by Kairo regular, Micro Lab, creator commentary, and unrelated personal post.

### TikTok individual video
Use profile > select the video > More insights (or More options > Analytics). The post analytics may expose retention curve, average watch time, watched full video, traffic sources, shares, comments and search impressions. Creator-side TikTok Studio > Analytics > Content is another route. Record the video duration and graph drop-off timestamps as well as the amount watched, not only views. Depending on account type and API availability, Metricool may show average watch time and full-video-watched rate, but it **cannot substitute** for timestamp-level in-app retention.

### YouTube Studio Shorts
YouTube Studio > Content > select Short > Analytics > Engagement: audience retention / average view duration. Studio > Analytics > Content > Shorts: stayed to watch / swiped away and traffic sources. Retention can take 1–2 days to process. Compare videos of like length.

### Threads
Profile > Insights > Views / Interactions / Audience and top posts; check followers vs non-followers, replies, reposts, quote posts and profile/link actions when available. Do not claim native second-by-second video retention from Threads Insights. Compare personal conversational posts to Kairo posts separately and test discussions specific to AI and cybersecurity.

## Success metrics
1. **Hold attention:** seconds watched / video duration; at 3 seconds and first meaningful drop; full-video-watched rate, per topic and length.
2. **Share / save / discuss:** video shares, comments, replies, reposts, questions per post; qualitative question quality.
3. **Convert:** profile visits, follows/subscribes, returning viewers, Kairo-specific discussion threads.
4. **Effort / consistency:** production cost per finished original, accessible media, truthful captions, review gate compliance.

## First four-week tests
- Week 1: two different accurately phrased first-three-second hooks in separate comparable videos; no fabricated urgency. Record early retention.
- Week 2: evidence-first demo or real screen-recorded Micro Lab versus narrative episode; compare completion and useful comments **within comparable length bands**.
- Week 3: first-person creator research note on Threads versus video-only announcement; compare replies, profile action, and Kairo-specific follower gains.
- Week 4: evaluate, keep one successful tested element, and change one other variable. Never overfit a single viral exception.

## Engagement response guardrails
- Reply thoughtfully to real questions where possible; use one genuine question at the end of relevant posts.
- On Threads, join relevant discussions in an informed, constructive voice, not repetitive promotional replies.
- Analytics/publishing may be handled by Metricool; this connected plugin currently does **not** expose reading or writing TikTok/Threads comments or inbox messages.
- No deceptive engagement, spam, synthetic comments posing as supporters, or autonomous replies without approval.
