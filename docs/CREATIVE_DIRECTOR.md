# Kairo Knox — Creative Director: Eight-Step Research-to-Learning Loop
Version: 2026-10-30 | Status: Proposed editorial operating standard (not automated software).

## Purpose and boundaries
Improve *useful* attention and audience trust across Core Episodes, Micro Learning, Signal Cuts, Instagram carousels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Threads. The objective is stronger openings, original analysis, more saved/shared practical value and substantive discussion, **not** guaranteed reach. Preserve Kairo's masked identity and existing rights, sourcing, MP4/ZIP verification and final human publishing approval in [CONTENT_WORKFLOW.md](CONTENT_WORKFLOW.md).

## The eight steps (run for each selected topic or batch)

### 1. Find relevant winners, not just high-view posts
- Start with a viewer problem and one niche (AI, cybersecurity, robotics, business, marketing, algorithms, investing concepts, gaming or society).
- Research **recent (~30 days where feasible)** publicly accessible, appropriately licensed or permitted reference content. Record author, date, URL, format, subject and *available* views, followers/reach and baseline; respect access restrictions and provider terms. Never claim SearchTok, Claude or an analytics connector is integrated unless verified.
- Prefer **outperformers relative to that creator's comparable content**, not just the largest accounts. If a baseline is missing, label performance "unverified" and use as inspiration only.
- Collect up to three candidates and describe the audience promise and differentiating idea. Do not reproduce copyrighted slide text, scripts or footage.

### 2. Analyze structure, not wording
For each example, identify hook mechanism, slide/scene count, tension, sequence of ideas, proof, payoff location, clarity, transition and discussion prompt. Document what *principle* works. Explicitly avoid copying distinctive wording, character design or layouts.

### 3. Generate and fact-check original hooks
Produce 10 original cover/opening options, generally under 10 words for carousel covers. Pick 2–3 that are understandable in the first 1–2 seconds and communicate a real consequence. Verify each implied factual promise; reject unsupported superlatives, fabricated outcomes, manipulative fear and misleading comparisons. An accurate, concrete hook beats vague mystery.

### 4. Make each slide/scene do one job
Default carousel blueprint: **7 slides** (adjust to 5–9 when the subject needs it), **one clear idea per slide**, generally **20 words or fewer of primary slide copy** (excluding source notes/alt text).
1. Cover: precise human/business consequence or practical promise.
2. Context: what the viewer needs to know.
3. Tension: what is changing, failing or misunderstood.
4. Evidence: chart, example or bounded demonstration with source.
5. Explanation: mechanism or relevant comparison.
6. Payoff: actionable takeaway, checklist or surprising but warranted synthesis.
7. Conversation: one specific, answerable question.
Each transition should reward continued attention, not conceal essential information. Video formats adapt the same logic to hook → evidence/demo → payoff → question; do not force seven cuts into a seven-second video.

### 5. Build save/share value
Ask which one or two slides someone would legitimately save, use, or screenshot. Improve the weakest two with a compact checklist, diagram, reproducible safe workflow, definition, comparison or source-backed decision rule. For Micro Learning, show meaningful inputs, outputs and limits. Useful beats artificially "viral."

### 6. End with meaningful conversation
Write one easy-to-answer but substantive question, grounded in the actual tradeoff or viewer experience. On Threads, invite multiple credible perspectives without manufacturing an outrage binary. Never write engagement-bait ("comment YES") or misrepresent evidence to increase replies.

### 7. Repurpose a *validated* idea, not identical files
Adapt winners based on evidence and learning, with independent editorial value:
- **TikTok**: visible concrete first-second consequence and fast evidence/demo.
- **YouTube Shorts**: searchable subject title and complete standalone takeaway.
- **Instagram Reel**: clear visuals and concise caption; **Instagram carousel**: 5–9 original slides with a useful screenshot-worthy takeaway.
- **Threads**: text-first tradeoff, verified build insight or discussion question, not an automatic video dump.
- **Signal Cuts**: distinct source-backed business/AI mechanism and human consequence, not a duplicate Core Episode.
- **Micro Learning**: separate practical demo where the topic supports it. Keep the two Core : one Micro Learning cadence.
Adapt title, cover, spoken script and CTA; reuse only assets and material with proper rights. A "winner" is an editorial hypothesis until actual performance is observed.

### 8. Evaluate and iterate, with three measurable experiments
Every week review the latest **10 eligible posts** (or all available if fewer), grouped by platform, format and comparable age. Use the definitions and N/A rules in [MEASUREMENT.md](MEASUREMENT.md). Capture watch/retention (for video), views/reach, saves, shares and *meaningful* replies. Compare to a same-account **median of comparable posts**; do not compare raw TikTok views to Threads reach. Note sample size, publication dates and missing data.
Produce three *testable* experiment proposals, e.g.:
- **Hook**: concrete human consequence vs abstract claim; compare three-second hold and normalized watch among comparable videos.
- **Carousel**: practical checklist on slide 5 vs slide 6; compare saves/reach among comparable carousels.
- **Threads**: evidence-led tradeoff vs broad question; compare substantive replies per 100 views, if views are available.
Change only one or two variables per test, label observations versus interpretation, choose a next action and do not assert statistical certainty from small samples.

## Mandatory Creative Director output per proposed piece
- Content lane, target viewer, one-sentence thesis, practical value.
- Source log (URLs, dates, verification status, rights status); comparison candidates and relative baseline if available.
- Ten original candidate hooks; selected hook and reasons.
- Seven-slide draft or equivalent scene-by-scene video plan, each element's purpose, evidence and payoff.
- Screenshot/save-worthy artifact, one discussion question, platform-native derivatives.
- Score: **truth/rights**, **hook clarity**, **human relevance**, **evidence/demo**, **save value**, **native fit**, **discussion quality** (0 = fails, 1 = needs work, 2 = good). Any 0 on truth/rights is a hard hold; other low scores require revision.
- Production, factual-rights, MP4/ZIP, user review and per-platform publishing approval status, **each separately tracked**.
- Measurements + three test hypotheses after performance data is available.

## Reusable prompt sequence (adapt, do not paste blindly)
1. "Find up to three recent standout posts in [niche] and compare each with that account's normal performance. Cite URLs and mark absent metrics N/A. Do not copy their wording."
2. "Analyze the structure: number of slides or scenes, hook, tension, proof, transition, payoff and last question."
3. "Write ten *original* truthful hooks under ten words each for [topic]."
4. "Draft seven slides, one idea each, generally under twenty words of primary slide copy; make each transition useful."
5. "Identify the two weakest slides and rewrite them to have a legitimate save/screenshot payoff."
6. "Write one short, good-faith, answerable question relevant to our viewer."
7. "Adapt the idea into separate TikTok, YouTube Short, Instagram and Threads versions; do not assume crossposting is appropriate."
8. "Assess the latest ten comparable posts, separate evidence from guesses, and recommend three measurable experiments."

## Editorial decision
Research `->` original pitch `->` source/rights gate `->` script/carousel `->` Creative Director score `->` verified render/packaging `->` explicit human approval `->` publication confirmation `->` measurement `->` next test.

**Implementation scope:** This document is a repeatable manual/agent-invokable specification. It does **not** install SearchTok, change social account settings, read private analytics automatically, create an active scheduled agent, or publish content.
