# Retention-First Production and Platform Packaging — Kairo Knox
Date: 2026-11-17. Companion to the open explanation-depth review. **This is a production standard, not a video render or live social post.** No public posting, paid rendering, or credit spending is authorized by this document.

## Shared narrative structure for all three educational lanes
**Opening 0–3 s:** Consequence or prediction, subject identifiable and stakes visually obvious. Show the problem before making the viewer sit through a branded intro. Test consequence-first versus prediction-first without misleading urgency.

**Setup:** Explain who is affected and what decision/result matters. Use natural speaking style and concrete terms rather than abstract declarations.

**Cause and effect:** Show an actual mechanism: input -> action -> changed state -> output. Use a safe illustrated demo, rights-cleared original screen capture, or sourced footage. Display a clear label for simulations, estimates or fiction. A decorative still image with camera pan does not meet this gate.

**Tension and proof:** Contrast outcomes, edge cases or counterexamples; distinguish verified observation from prediction and third-party report.

**Payoff and meaningful close:** Deliver a repeatable checklist, recognition cue, decision tradeoff or concrete understanding. End with one answerable relevant question; do not append generic engagement bait.

Use motivated scene transitions, not arbitrary rapid cutting. An explanatory 30–60 second episode is acceptable when it contains real demonstration and narrative progression. Duration is subordinate to a satisfying explanation.

## Lane-specific expectations
| Lane | Opening | On-screen learning | Final value |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Micro Learning | Immediate real-world problem, e.g. an account stays signed in | Safe before/after demonstration with visible UI state, diagram or code-first visualization | Viewer can repeat a check or explain a mechanism |
| Signal Kut | Unexpected industry consequence framed with fair evidence | Competing explanations, reported-vs-verified split and clear numeric/source labels | Viewer understands the decision/implication, not just the headline |
| Kairo Knox Core | A compelling personal/business or technological consequence | Cinematic investigation with linked scenes, distinct narrative arc and a credible test or example | Earned insight and an unresolved but meaningful question |

Chronicles/Facebook fiction use the existing separate story/acting standards. Do not turn their distinct cast into an education-format slideshow or treat fictional narrative as factual documentary.

## Mandatory creative review (before any external credit or master render)
1. Two clearly different truthful hooks, intended viewer, one-sentence audience reason to care.
2. Visual storyboard includes exact opening subject and stakes by second 3.
3. At least one fully specified demonstration: input, change, outcome, and limits. Each key scene has a job.
4. Factual claims link to dated sources; illustrative data clearly marked; approved rights for every third-party asset and sound.
5. Existing original Kairo masked identity and approved voice continuity, without metallic distortion or stitched mismatched lines.
6. Distinct platform package for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and (only when appropriate) a **native conversation** on Threads. No generic date titles or identical long copy across services.
7. A single predeclared creative variable (e.g. first three-second hook), comparable post-age review window, and a defined primary metric. Never interpret an ordinary Instagram Trial Reel comparison as a randomized experiment.

## Mandatory final audio/video review (never infer from a script)
- Fully rendered MP4 exists, decodes, and matches approved shot/caption manifest and final version.
- Watch the whole episode on a phone; check 9:16 safe zones, text contrast and subtitle accuracy.
- Listen to the whole audio-only track for consistent voice, pronunciation, realistic cadence, clipped/robotic speech, timing, ambience and clean sound transitions. Visually inspect voice/lip-sync where present.
- Verify scene-to-scene motion, character identity and camera geography; no frozen AI-generated slides, incorrect hands/faces or discontinuous action.
- For coded animations, check the actual full-length frame-driven render. For character scenes, actual acting motion and synchronized performances are required.
- Inspect actual rights/source labels and MP4 integrity; archive immutable checksum and independent reviewer notes.
- If incomplete, mark HOLD and document the exact missing item. **Do not turn a successful visual draft into a claim of completed audio/master QC.**

## Four-platform editorial packaging
**Instagram:** Show the visual consequence instantly; strong compact cover; caption opens with concrete context, not a pile of tags. Test three-second skip rate first, then watch time, shares and saves.

**TikTok:** Fast legible setup, unique natural caption, search-ready terminology; gather native viewer retention and source data. Connector missing post metrics is **unknown**, not zero. Review actual uploaded post presence before diagnosing a channel.

**YouTube Shorts:** Descriptive specific title rather than a date; upload details tailored to actual viewer search, clear useful thumbnail/first frame and verifiable demonstration. Confirm actual video IDs and native viewed-vs-swiped/retention.

**Threads:** No bulk auto-promo or copy/pasted caption. Start a sincere question, a first-person build lesson or evidence-backed tradeoff, maintain personality, and measure replies. Separate personal entertainment posts from Kairo-specific conversations; never claim unrelated personal traction proves Kairo demand.

## Two-week pilot (approval dependent)
Six original videos: **two Micro Learning, two Signal Kut, two Core Kairo Knox**. No more than one Signal Kut per week initially. Produce in batches but space primary same-platform release slots by roughly 24–48 hours, with the established 3–4 maximum baseline a flexible ceiling. This plan is **not scheduled** and dates/times cannot override final approval.

Each proposed cut needs a small record:
- content_id, lane, intended viewer, hypothesis, 2 hook variants and selected hook
- effect visible in first 3 seconds, demonstrative input/change/outcome/limits, source links
- storyboard and planned seconds, asset/voice IDs, full rights and cost status
- title/caption/cover per platform, published ID and URL only after verified posting
- target post age 48 hours ±8, view threshold and primary measurement predeclared before the trial
- dated native/Metricool observation; mark absent TikTok metrics and YouTube per-video fields unknown
- review after 24h, 48h and 7d; group like format/platform/length; note outliers and report median, not a viral exception

**Starting hypotheses, not performance guarantees:** stronger demo-with-consequence opening will lower Instagram three-second skip rate; a real build problem with a sincere question will produce more substantive Threads replies than a product announcement; descriptive YouTube titles and clearer openings may improve discoverability and watched-vs-swiped.
