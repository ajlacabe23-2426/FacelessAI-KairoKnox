## Production sequence (one controlled change per iteration)
1. Story lock: canon comparison with actual prior published cut, logline, roles, location map, stakes, emotional arc, ending; director signs storyboard and runtime. No media spending yet.
2. Asset lock: cast reference sheets, environment views, props, faction silhouette/icon/sound distinctions, exact reference asset IDs, rights and voice permissions. Distinguish characters without relying on color alone.
3. Animatic: voice-first timing using approved performances or explicitly labeled scratch audio; storyboard panels, rough sound and intentional silence. Check every transition and change of location. A still animatic is never the release.
4. Blind comprehension gate: reviewer who has not read the script answers: where are we; what does Nyra want; who opposes her and why; why is VESPER different; what changed in the confrontation; what is unresolved? All six must be correctly understood; unclear answer triggers targeted rewrite.
5. Shot generation: generate only approved shot IDs with references, start/end pose or frame, screen geography, dialogue timing, sound intent and continuity anchor. Compare adjacent shots before locking. Keep a human-readable source-to-cut map with actual IDs and time ranges in ignored private records.
6. Editorial: lock accepted shots; keep a change ledger (shot, defect, acceptance test, cost). Reuse existing footage only when it passes shot and continuity gates. Do not create another whole-episode version for a local defect. One current candidate, prior version archived. No unapproved paid retries.
7. Sound: distinct voices, environmental beds per location, motivated score, impacts tied to visible animation, intentional quiet before key revelations. Review with sound and muted; captions preserve canonical names.
8. QA: full uninterrupted playback (desktop and phone), coherent setting/characters/story, motion and lip-sync, dialogue clarity, safe framing, verified unwatermarked rights-cleared media, exact exported duration, checksum, reviewer/date/defects; no metadata-only approval. Verify the actual master and private review route. A green structural check does not certify artistic quality.
9. Release: only after explicit final director approval, approved platform copy, and existing distribution gates. No autonomous publication. Maintain genuinely ready Micro Learning and Signal Kuts in independent lanes; no invented schedules or posts.
