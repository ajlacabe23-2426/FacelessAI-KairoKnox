## Non-negotiable production process
1. **Inventory**: source animations (not Descript editorial outputs), locked character reference images, approved voices, scenery, previous Ep001 continuity frame, music/license provenance. Record sources and rights.
2. **Storyboard lock**: Facebook 46s comedy beats and Kairo 85s eight shots. A story edit is a new version requiring director approval.
3. **Voice-first**: distinct fictional character voices, actor-style timing and scene breaths; lay voice track before full motion generation. Ainsley narrator preference applies to Kairo narrator if available.
4. **Motion pilot**: generate ONE fully animated 6–8s pilot per series; inspect actor motion, lip-sync and continuity before any large batch. Do not pay for batch until pilots meet acceptance.
5. **Scene production**: generate contiguous physically coherent shots; preserve camera geography, identities, costumes and lighting continuity.
6. **Edit+mix**: cut on emotion/action; natural dialogue timing; room tone, foley, restrained music; verify audio peaks and dialogue intelligibility.
7. **Delivery**: original, unwatermarked 1080x1920 vertical MP4 if native/authorized source permits; captioned master + captions SRT + cover + asset and licensing manifest + per-platform titles/captions + archive. 720p upscale alone is not native 1080p.
8. **Independent QA**: physically review completed video, not metadata or script; assess every speaking shot for real facial/body motion and lip-sync; verify all words/names/captions and safe area, no unintended frozen-frame stretches, no watermark, original identities, licensing.
9. **Private final approval**: final playable mastered file, *then* user director review. Public/social publishing remains locked until explicit approval and verified platform acceptance.
