# Research and editorial policy

This channel prioritizes uncommon, verifiable AI developments over broad, sensational headlines. Editorial judgment belongs to the creator.

## Standard workflow
1. Start with a research question, not a predetermined conclusion.
2. Record the ORIGINAL source, author or institution, publication date, and separate event date.
3. Read primary material: reports, papers, official records or reproducible demonstrations. Secondary reporting may add context.
4. Map each factual claim to a source ID; mark interpretation or hypothetical questions separately.
5. Check the methods, sample, evaluated models, limitations and conflicting evidence.
6. Recheck time-sensitive claims before recording and again before publishing.
7. Write scripts in original language, with links rather than long reproductions of source texts.
8. Time narration and perform a final human factual and visual review.

## Accuracy guardrails
- Evaluation awareness does not demonstrate consciousness.
- Performing an unintended action is not automatic proof of an enduring secret objective or deception.
- Lab or evaluation results are not evidence that a behavior occurred at scale in production.
- Prefer precise language: reported, observed in evaluation, tested, suggested, not established.
- Avoid fabricated quotations, statistics, screenshots, citations or media.

## Publication gate
- [ ] Creator has reviewed the primary source(s)
- [ ] Claim ledger matches narration
- [ ] Limitations and uncertainty are preserved
- [ ] Dates and AI model versions verified
- [ ] Narration timing and approved Kairo asset checked
- [ ] Final render reviewed by creator
- [ ] Publication approved intentionally

## Corrections
Log the original wording, what was wrong, the evidence, correction date and the affected platforms. Correct public posts or add a clearly identified correction when needed. Never quietly replace a factual assertion without recording the change.

Automation can organize and draft, but cannot certify a study as read or published without a human check.
