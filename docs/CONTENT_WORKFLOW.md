# Kairo Knox — Production and Publishing Workflow
Version: 2026-10-30. Editorial policy; not an automatic publishing job.

## Mission and lanes
- **Core Episodes**: researched AI, technology, cybersecurity, robotics and societal implications.
- **Micro Learning**: one practical, demonstrable, repeatable lesson for every two regular Core Episodes. Show meaningful inputs, outputs, limitations, and safety boundaries.
- **Signal Cuts**: a distinct original editorial series linking AI with business, algorithms, advertising, investing concepts, entertainment and gaming, consumer behavior, economics, and society. Do not simply repost or repackage the same Kairo episode. Use source material only with appropriate rights and meaningful original commentary.
- **Kairo Knox: Chronicles**: an additional original fictional animated adventure lane. Cyberpunk dark Art Deco + mecha-anime-influenced worldbuilding, clearly labeled fiction, featuring adult human/machine conflict with non-graphic action and moral consequences. Follow [series bible](CHRONICLES_SERIES_BIBLE.md) and [pilot 001](CHRONICLES_PILOT_001.md). It does not replace the existing lanes, and it requires locked original characters, shot-level continuity reviews, separate spending estimates and explicit final publishing approval.
- **Kairo Debate Drops (Threads-first)**: original 1–3 sentence prompts identifying meaningful tradeoffs in AI, entertainment, games, technology, marketing, business and society. These are text-led conversations, not another video quota. Valuable debates can inspire optional evidence-checked Threads-native video proposals, subject to review, source-rights and final publication approval.

**Chronicles is not news footage.** Label fictional animation as such, avoid borrowed franchise-specific visual designs and music, and keep fictional world events separate from sourced technology claims.

Maintain Kairo Knox's consistent masked analyst identity and established visual/audio language. Reuse approved assets first. Higgsfield may be used for approved production. Use established voice profiles as specified for an individual piece; no blanket permission to purchase credits.

## Creative Director research and packaging loop
Before pitching a carousel, episode, Micro Learning or Signal Cut, follow the eight-step [Creative Director standard](CREATIVE_DIRECTOR.md): study relevant, appropriately sourced comparable successes; identify their *structure*; write original accurate hooks; give each slide/scene a distinct job; build a real save-worthy payoff; ask a substantive question; plan platform-native adaptations; and evaluate measured results rather than assuming virality. Treat outside cover wording as a pattern to analyze, **not text to copy**. For Instagram carousels, default to seven short slides but adapt to topic complexity. Record source dates, permissions and missing metrics honestly.

## Mandatory stage gates
1. **Discover & pitch**: topic, intended viewer, one-sentence claim, audience benefit, format, evidence plan.
2. **Evidence & rights**: verify all factual assertions using trustworthy sources, distinguish reported results from independently validated results, capture source URLs and publication dates, confirm rights/permissions for any outside video, audio, logos and images. Credit sources where appropriate. A transformation or attribution alone does not automatically confer rights.
3. **Script**: write a compelling truthful first 1–2 seconds; a clearly identifiable question/tension; useful development; qualified takeaway; one substantive viewer question. Avoid sensational unsupported certainty or engagement bait.
4. **Production**: approved character reference, established branding and voice, subtitles, phone-safe framing, intelligible narration; check timing, aspect ratio, frames and audio. Preserve the user-approved material and keep drafts separate from approved outputs.
5. **Review cut**: render and verify the MP4 plus a ZIP of finished deliverables. Inspect playability and content integrity. Do not claim the artifacts exist without checking them.
6. **Final human approval**: user reviews the finished cut and platform copy. No automatic publishing, post creation, or approval bypass. A prior idea approval is not final publishing approval.
7. **Platform packaging**: tailor captions/cover/title/tags for each platform; verify links; consult the distribution rules in PLATFORM_PLAYBOOK.md.
8. **Publish & document**: only after explicit final approval, post through a supported connected account or supply a manual-publish package. Save post URL and timestamp in the release log; do not assert success unless provider confirms.
9. **Measure & iterate**: check outcomes at approximately 24 hours, 48 hours and 7 days when data is available. Compare to comparable past posts and adjust ONE or TWO variables at a time. See MEASUREMENT.md.
10. **Archive**: keep organized finished, approved assets and release metadata in their designated repository locations; avoid uploading unfinished project footage, private account details, secrets or unlicensed source clips.

## Scheduling guardrails: experimental, not algorithm claims
- Video-platform starting cadence: **3–4 primary video releases per platform per week**, not 3–4 across all platforms combined.
- Core rotation remains **two episodes : one Micro Learning**. Insert **at most one Signal Cut weekly** until its pilot performance is reviewed; adjust the overall release count rather than piling onto the usual cadence.
- Aim for **24–48 hours between primary releases on the same platform**. Timing and frequency are hypotheses; they are not evidence of algorithmic suppression.
- Do not release multiple freshly produced titles in the same hour on a single video platform merely because they are ready.
- Chronicles may be produced as an independent pilot, but any release must fit the same limited posting capacity and receive per-platform approval. Do not silently spend the remaining Higgsfield credit balance on a complete action sequence; test a short shot, review, then budget the remaining work.
- Produce in batches independently of distribution. Production capacity must not force publishing frequency.
- Prioritize the limited Metricool monthly posting allowance for high-value releases. Never schedule merely to occupy an open slot.
- Proposed Signal Cuts pilot windows (America/Chicago, NOT scheduled): **Oct 30 at 10:00 AM; Nov 4 at 6:00 PM; Nov 9 at 10:00 AM**. Move a window if a Core Episode or Micro Learning would collide or final review is incomplete.
- Threads follows a **separate conversation-first calendar** described in PLATFORM_PLAYBOOK.md: roughly two Debate Drops, a verifiable build log, and a practical insight per week, with occasional additional conversation if warranted. Do not auto-crosspost videos to Threads.
- Debate responses may inform a **research and pitch queue**, never an automated production or publication action. A Threads-native video derived from a discussion requires up-to-date evidence checks, rights clearance, the established review-cut gate, and final user approval.
- Publication remains subject to the user's final approval for each video and post.

## Preflight quality checklist
- [ ] Creative Director brief prepared with original hooks, useful payoff and platform-specific plan
- [ ] Opening actually communicates the subject within 2 seconds
- [ ] Every numerical and scientific claim sourced and caveated where required
- [ ] Clip rights or licenses and attribution reviewed
- [ ] No private client data, internal tokens, credentials, or exploitable live security details
- [ ] Kairo identity, voice, styling, captions, and vertical crop coherent
- [ ] Rendered MP4/ZIP verified and ready to inspect
- [ ] Each platform caption is distinct and readable
- [ ] Final user review and publishing approval obtained
- [ ] Release does not crowd another primary post
- [ ] Publish result and measurement checkpoints logged
