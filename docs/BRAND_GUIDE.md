# Kairo Knox — brand guide

**Series:** Beneath the System.
**Identity:** original futuristic masked AI analyst; investigative, technically grounded, human voice.
**Character baseline:** the previously approved BLACK Kairo design used in Episode 1. The exact master reference asset is not present in this repo. Do not generate a lookalike and claim it is identical. Obtain approval against the real asset first.
**Narration baseline:** Fraser voice chosen by creator; controlled, thoughtful, cinematic, never melodramatic.
**Visuals:** deep blacks, cool cyan/blue holographic displays; sparse red for actual warning moments. Slow pushes, gentle head turns, subtle mask pulses. Avoid visual drift between episodes.
**Output:** vertical 9:16; usually 60–90 seconds; EP03 aims at 75 seconds. Phone-legible captions and accurate source/date panels.
**Brand lock:** KAIRO KNOX — BENEATH THE SYSTEM.
**Principle:** abstraction is okay; misleading fake incident footage is not.

Keep character master images, narrator source audio and large production projects in private media storage, along with provenance and license information. Do not place them in the current public repository.

## Creator-behind-the-mask direction (approved October 25, 2026)

**Core positioning:** Turn Kairo Knox into a recognizable AI and cybersecurity channel that combines compelling stories with demonstrations people can learn from, while using Threads for thoughtful conversations and community building. We cannot guarantee a viral video; we can build a repeatable process to discover what genuinely earns attention.

Kairo remains the recognizable original black-masked on-screen analyst. The real creator is gradually revealed through genuine research judgments, narration, hands-on testing, work-in-progress lessons, voice/personality and optional faceless behind-the-scenes clips. Do **not** quietly replace the mask, invent a human identity, or require a real name, face, location, family detail, workplace, or private account link. Any deeper identity disclosure requires specific creator approval.

### Progressive creative integration
1. **Next two regular episodes — Investigator:** introduce the researcher's personal viewpoint (e.g. "I tested this", "Here's the evidence I found") only when the test/research actually happened. Keep the original mask, Fraser narrator and cinematic continuity.
2. **Following two or three episodes — Builder:** show real opt-in screen recordings, successful/failed lab results, small build challenges, and measured lessons. Redact accounts, security identifiers and private information before public footage.
3. **Creator phase — Optional:** let the creator identify their role as the person behind Kairo through perspective and carefully selected glimpses, while preserving the mysterious persona. Never set or assume a face-reveal deadline.

### Editorial and distribution
- Maintain **two regular Kairo episodes per one hands-on Micro Lab**. Every learning clip must deliver a testable viewer takeaway.
- Use **TikTok / YouTube Shorts** for immediate hook, evidence, on-screen experiment, implications, and next action.
- Use **Threads** for creator-perspective context, nuance, open-ended technical questions and genuinely responsive discussion, not merely duplicate video captions.
- Every post stays subject to actual finished-video creator sign-off before publication, with distinct platform copy and verified URLs.
- Use real experiences and honest uncertainties. Do not fabricate claims like "I ran this test" when it was a sourced third-party experiment.
