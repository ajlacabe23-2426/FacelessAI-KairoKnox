# Kairo Knox — brand guide

**Series:** Beneath the System.
**Identity:** original futuristic masked AI analyst; investigative, technically grounded, human voice.
**Character baseline:** the previously approved BLACK Kairo design used in Episode 1. The exact master reference asset is not present in this repo. Do not generate a lookalike and claim it is identical. Obtain approval against the real asset first.
**Narration baseline:** Fraser voice chosen by creator; controlled, thoughtful, cinematic, never melodramatic.
**Visuals:** deep blacks, cool cyan/blue holographic displays; sparse red for actual warning moments. Slow pushes, gentle head turns, subtle mask pulses. Avoid visual drift between episodes.
**Output:** vertical 9:16; usually 60–90 seconds; EP03 aims at 75 seconds. Phone-legible captions and accurate source/date panels.
**Brand lock:** KAIRO KNOX — BENEATH THE SYSTEM.
**Principle:** abstraction is okay; misleading fake incident footage is not.

Keep character master images, narrator source audio and large production projects in private media storage, along with provenance and license information. Do not place them in the current public repository.
