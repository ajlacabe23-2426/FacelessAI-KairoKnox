# Editorial-first roadmap

## Milestone 1: foundation (this pull request)
Episode registry, source policy, character guide, EP03 claim ledger, draft narration, shot plan and structural CI.

## Milestone 2: recover originals
Import or reference authentic EP01 and EP02 scripts; verify their research rather than recreating from assumptions. Verify the approved Kairo black reference. Read all EP03 original sources and time Fraser narration.

## Milestone 3: assisted pipeline
Optional source intake and deduplication, proposal templates, evidence status, source-date tracking, script and production checklists. Keep topic selection and editorial conclusions human-directed.

## Milestone 4: production integrations
Only after permissions, pricing and reliability are checked: connect suitable generation and editing tools. Do not assume GitHub can render or publish video.

## Milestone 5: measurement
Track confirmed public URLs, view retention, feedback and corrections. Refine style without turning into a clickbait news feed.

## Non-goals
No autonomous publication, no reconstruction of licensed media, no speculative claim of completed work, no cross-project secrets.
