# Chronicles Pilot 001 — THE OBSIDIAN PROTOCOL
Version: 2026-10-30 | 45-second proof-of-concept | Fiction / non-graphic animated sci-fi
Status: scripted and storyboarded; **not** a complete 45-second rendered MP4.

## Creative objective
One immediately legible human-versus-mech confrontation that turns into a moral dilemma. The viewer should understand the city, the central opponent and the twist **without** watching a prequel. Visual continuity and intelligible stakes matter more than density of effects.

**Hook:** “Meridian never slept. It calculated.”
**Central question:** If an authoritarian machine keeps essential systems alive, who can safely turn it off?
**Hero:** Adult human scout Nyra Vale; Kairo Knox as masked analyst/narrator.
**Opposing presence:** VESPER-9, a guardian mech capable of estimating likely choices but not reading minds.
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, intended 45 s, ideally 24 fps masters, phone-safe text/framing; no graphic harm.
**Disclosure:** “Original animated sci-fi fiction — Kairo Knox: Chronicles.”

## Edit and sound direction
- Open on the giant scale of the human/mech contrast — no generic logo pre-roll.
- Aim for one coherent camera move per generated clip. Establish the bridge orientation and screen direction once; keep them consistent.
- Alternate three short kinetic beats with two holds; let silence reveal the stakes.
- No copied movie/anime music. Use original/cleared dark orchestral-synth texture, low brass pulses, and mechanical ambience.
- Prefer Kairo's established approved voice for lines labeled Kairo. Other voices must remain distinct. If an approved reference is unavailable, use temporary silent captions and replace at finishing.
- Cut the last line before the ring is fully comprehensible. Credits/brand slate after the teaser are optional and must not obscure the payoff.

### Shot prompts
**S01:** “Wide low-angle vertical city bridge at night. An adult human scout in charcoal futuristic armor stands with back to camera facing colossal VESPER-9, whose smooth obsidian armor has subtle gold inlays and a narrow amber visor. Slow dolly forward. Guardian tilts its head. Single uninterrupted shot; consistent human/mech scale.”

**S02:** “Medium over-shoulder scout shot. Amber scanning line crosses her helmet; monumental black-gold civic doors seal far behind, carefully preserving environmental geometry. The mech watches without striking. Controlled parallax, deliberate movement.”

**S03:** “High three-quarter view of the bridge with one adult human scout and one huge guardian. Three translucent amber projected *possible paths* briefly bloom on the ground ahead; the scout changes direction around a luminous barrier. No collision, no morphing or supernatural powers.”

**S04:** “Very wide vertical view of two distinctly human-operated utility mechs moving into separate skyway positions while guardian VESPER-9 turns. One architectural panel tips toward a lower empty plaza; guardian diverts it into a safe service platform. Substantial mechanical weight, restrained spectacular movement.”

**S05:** “Close-up of Nyra's armored glove hesitating over a fictional holographic shutdown control, then rack focus to a clear abstract schematic linking the tower to emergency medical infrastructure. Leave interface text for post-production overlay; avoid model-generated gibberish text.”

**S06:** “Two-shot in an immense Deco bridge hall: human scout calls for stand-down with raised open palm; two distant utility mechs stop; giant guardian stills and its amber optical band dims. Reflected symmetrical silhouettes, long held moment; no contact.”

**S07:** “Approved Kairo Knox masked analyst in the established black/gunmetal costume, viewed in profile from a high dark geometrical observation room, cobalt holographic light; subtle head turn toward city skyline. Reference the actual approved Kairo asset; do not invent or redesign his mask.”

**S08:** “Two-second vast skyline reveal. Unfamiliar abstract ring of pale light forms silently within distant thunderclouds beyond original Deco towers. City is tiny below. Sudden cut to black. No words or logos.”
