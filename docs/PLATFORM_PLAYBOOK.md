# Kairo Knox — Platform-Specific Editorial Playbook
Version: 2026-10-27. Strategy is tailored, not a universal algorithm workaround.

## Audience roles
| Platform | Primary role | Typical content | Default call to action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TikTok | Discovery and curiosity | Strong hook, brief demonstration or analysis, visual payoff | One thoughtful question |
| YouTube Shorts | Searchable evergreen lessons and discovery | Accurate titled explainer or demo, strong first seconds | Related lesson or question |
| Instagram Reels | Visual storytelling and sharing | Polished Kairo visuals, self-contained idea and subtitles | Share or discuss genuinely useful insight |
| Threads (personal account) | Conversation, documentation, credibility, genuine debate | Short Kairo Debate Drops, documented build logs, technical decisions and informal discussion | Invite a reasoned perspective or counterexample |
| GitHub | Verifiable evidence and method | Public-safe technical changelogs, demo/CI evidence, research notes and workflow | Link only to information approved to share |

## Threads: discussion and build-documentation first
**Do not auto-publish the Core Episode, Micro Learning or Signal Cut MP4 to Threads.** Threads is its own editorial surface; video may appear occasionally when a native explanation or demonstration is specifically compelling and the user approves it.

Starting mix per week: **2 short Kairo Debate Drops, 1 verified project/build update, 1 practical insight or technical question**, with up to 1 optional entertainment, gaming or business conversation (3–5 original text-led posts, adjusting for engagement and available time). These are targets, not fixed quotas; do not flood Threads to meet them.

A Threads post should:
1. Start with a concrete observation, decision, tradeoff, surprising test outcome, or sincere question.
2. Show evidence or context in plain language, without inflated PASS or release-readiness claims. Label **verified, reported, unverified, and next check** distinctly.
3. Explain *why the technical detail matters* to a person, team or business.
4. End with an answerable open-ended question rather than a generic “thoughts?”
5. When possible, reply to relevant comments and contribute to other threads instead of broadcasting only.
6. Allow room for human-interest topics like games, anime, work, technology culture and humor when authentic to the account; do not force all personal discussion to sound like brand advertising.

Example categories:
- **Build log**: What changed in Axiovela or a SaaS project, what passed, what remains unverified, and why it matters.
- **Security tradeoff**: What would you block from release if a security tool returned an incomplete result?
- **Product question**: Is a beautiful demo or fault-tolerant persistence more valuable for a first customer?
- **Economics/AI**: Who benefits when recommendation engines become much better at predicting consumer decisions?
- **Gaming systems**: How do reward loops and recommendation algorithms overlap in business and gaming?
- **Entertainment and media**: When AI can generate or adapt stories, what qualities should human creators retain control over?
- **Marketing and advertising**: When personalization works extremely well, where should consent and transparency begin?

## Kairo Debate Drops — short-form discussion designed for meaningful disagreement
**Purpose:** invite thoughtful, genuinely different perspectives and learn which intersections of entertainment, business, AI, gaming, technology and society merit further original coverage.

**Post shape (usually 1–3 short sentences):**
1. A concrete observation or imaginative but clearly labeled hypothetical.
2. Two plausible priorities or viewpoints in tension (never a dishonest false binary).
3. One sharp, open question asking people **why**, **under what conditions**, or **what would change their mind**.

**Sample prompts (adapt, don't repost mechanically):**
- "If AI can run a small business's marketing and customer support, what still makes that business special?"
- "Would you rather play a human-written game or one whose storyline changes intelligently for every player? What would make the experience better?"
- "If AI can generate nearly unlimited entertainment, does human originality become more valuable or less valuable?"
- "Should people be able to opt out when a company uses AI to personalize prices or advertising?"
- "When an AI tool makes a convincing demo in minutes, how much testing should be required before a business relies on it?"

**Debate guardrails:** Do not invent facts or pretend there are only two views when there are more. Do not bait hostility, encourage personal attacks, stereotype groups, impersonate participants, or exploit divisive current events purely for engagement. Keep it civil and curious; moderate harmful replies as appropriate. Present genuine counterpoints and avoid treating engagement as endorsement.

**From conversation to content (not automatic):**
1. Post an approved question; review replies over approximately 24–72 hours.
2. Note *themes* of arguments and unanswered questions without publishing commenters' private information.
3. If a distinct idea recurs, write a short text follow-up presenting two or more fair interpretations and a useful new question.
4. If the topic supports genuinely useful visuals, independently research current entertainment, business and AI developments and **propose an occasional original short video** (not merely a recycled Kairo episode).
5. Verify timeliness, sources, context, rights and nonmisleading footage; prepare platform-native script/caption.
6. Obtain **separate user approval for production and final publication**. No automatic video creation/publishing triggered by a comment count.

**Video frequency:** occasional, rather than another mandatory content quota; begin with no more than one Threads-native discussion video roughly every two weeks, and only when warranted by the conversation and compatible with the established Kairo release capacity. Threads remains text-first.

**Engagement:** When practical, respond with useful follow-up questions, respectfully steelman good opposing views and make room for organic non-tech discussion. Do not steer unrelated personal entertainment interests into constant product promotion.

Use a relevant native Threads topic tag when helpful. Do not pad with repeated hashtags, post identical video promo copy, expose proprietary facts, or overfill the feed.

## Captions and discovery
- Lead with an accurate subject-specific hook and clear audience benefit; front-load the first ~120 characters as an editorial guideline, **not** a platform character rule.
- Use naturally occurring topical words in the spoken video, on-screen title, description and caption: e.g., “AI agents”, “access controls”, “customer data isolation”, “marketing algorithms”, “consumer behavior”.
- One primary idea per caption, 1 evidence-backed payoff, one meaningful question. Avoid keyword stuffing and misleading claims.
- Instagram Reels: 2–5 narrowly relevant hashtags within the platform's maximum five, with none required. Do not expect tags alone to create reach.
- TikTok: start by testing 1–3 relevant tags; evaluate actual search traffic before adjusting.
- Threads: prefer an appropriate topic and clean prose over a hashtag block.
- YouTube Shorts: use a human-readable, searchable title and description, plus a minimal set of relevant tags only if useful.
- Do **not** copy the same long caption to all platforms.

## Human-only Instagram settings audit (not remotely changed)
The settings below are optional discovery/sharing choices, **not** proven ranking hacks. Confirm the current location of each control in the app because menus and availability change:
- [ ] Profile/account suggestion eligibility enabled, if available and desired.
- [ ] Sharing to Stories and repost options enabled, if desired.
- [ ] Embed permissions enabled only for material approved for public reuse/embedding.
- [ ] Public search-engine indexing enabled **only if** comfortable with externally indexed content and if the option is available for this account.
- [ ] Account visibility and safety/privacy tradeoffs explicitly understood.
Do not mark these as completed without inspecting the settings on the actual account.

## Publication safeguards
- Maintain the user's explicit review gate before any public post.
- Platform-specific captions, covers and metadata are separate release artifacts.
- No automatic Threads distribution just because a video was published on TikTok.
- Do not reuse third-party “viral clips” without clearing rights and checking context.
- If a provider fails to publish, record the error and verify the retry; do not claim success until confirmed.
