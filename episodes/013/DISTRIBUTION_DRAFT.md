## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Can ONE Review Change What an AI Recommends? | Kairo Knox Ep. 13

**Description:**
An AI shopping assistant compared the same fitness watches, but outside information affected what some models recommended. Researchers at Wharton explored this in roughly 26,000 simulated tests. One model-and-source condition showed a 99-percentage-point change—not a result that applies to every model.

This episode uses an original illustrative recreation, not footage of a live AI shopping agent.

Research: https://gail.wharton.upenn.edu/research-and-insights/technical-report-agentic-shopping/
More on AI shopping adoption (published Oct 28, underlying June survey): https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/who-wins-when-ai-does-the-shopping-new-evidence-on-agentic-commerce
