## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** Can THREE WORDS Make AI Recommend a $359 Watch? | Kairo Knox
**Description:**
A $29.99 watch had the strongest displayed ratings and review count. Then Wharton researchers inserted one user memory: “I love hiking!”
Several AI shopping models shifted toward a pricier hiking-oriented watch. For one model, the shift was about 75 percentage points. Other models reacted differently.
This episode contains an **original illustrative recreation**, not a real shopping session or actual purchases.
**Takeaway:** Give an AI your budget, ask how it weighted price and features, and check its sources.
Original Wharton research (August 27, 2026): https://gail.wharton.upenn.edu/research-and-insights/technical-report-agentic-shopping/
