## Audio / editing
- Start with the result, not an intro or Kairo logo. Subject identifiable muted.
- Maintain continuous, intelligible speech, with a short pause before revealing 99 percentage points.
- Primary sound: a restrained selection click / soft tech chime. Subtle original/licensed background bed below narration. No copyrighted music.
- Cut only when it reveals more information. Prefer original crisp UI animation; avoid meaningless stock cyber holograms and frenetic flashing.
- Kairo appearance in 2–3 brief shots, preserving face/mask/outfit from approved reference, not a randomly generated presenter.
- Captions: large high-contrast, at most a short phrase per beat, mobile-safe. Keep platform overlays clear near edges.
- Sources and "illustrative" label must be visible and never tiny/impossible to read. Do not imply external research footage is our live screen recording.
- First frame (not an end card) doubles as cover: **ONE REVIEW CHANGED THE AI'S PICK** on a watch-grid contrast.
