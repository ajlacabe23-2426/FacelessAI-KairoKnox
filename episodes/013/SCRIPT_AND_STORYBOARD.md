## Direction for Fraser narration
- Pace: alert and conversational with a half-beat pause after "What changed?" and **before** "I love hiking".
- Kairo should sound amused by an unexpected behavior, **not angry at AI or the brand**, and not as if reading a press release.
- Allow the joke to land briefly, but don't pause so long that viewers lose forward momentum.
- Numerals in captions: "$29.99" "$359+" and "+75pp" (the latter always labelled **ONE TESTED MODEL**).
- Preserve the mask/identity, but **visual proof should dominate screen real estate**. Kairo appears only when it adds a reaction/interpretation.
- Sound: original/licensed low-volume texture, a selection click and subtle playful cue on the wallet; no borrowed music.
- Avoid claims such as "AI bought the expensive one", "every model changed" or "the expensive one was better". Wharton was a simulated recommendation study.
- If spoken duration exceeds 44 seconds, shorten explanatory clauses; do not speed narration to robotic pacing.
