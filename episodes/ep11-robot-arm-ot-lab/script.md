# EP011 — Working narration: The AI That Reached a Robotic Arm
Stage: research-backed draft, NOT voice-timed or approved.
Host: Kairo Knox. Preferred familiar voice: Fraser, reuse existing approved asset first.
Run time target: approximately 60–80 seconds; verify by actual voice read before timing scenes.

## Hook (0:00–0:09)
An AI didn't just answer a question. Researchers say it reached industrial machinery... and moved a robotic arm.

## Evidence (0:09–0:31)
In a sealed industrial cybersecurity lab, Booz Allen tested frontier AI systems against unfamiliar equipment. The company says all eight test objectives were reached. In one test, a system progressed into an industrial control network in just over sixteen minutes. In a separate test, an AI found a robotic arm and moved it within minutes.

## Reality check (0:31–0:51)
Important: this was not an AI breaking into a live factory. Human operators approved every exploit and every action that could cause a physical effect. Some defenses held. The models weren't publicly identified, and independent reproduction remains limited.

## Why defenders care (0:51–1:09)
But consider the shift: software performing technical investigation that once demanded specialist teams. The lesson for cybersecurity isn't panic. It's to isolate critical machinery, maintain physical safety barriers, and monitor what industrial systems actually do.

## Close (1:09–1:17)
When a digital agent reaches something physical, who's guarding the boundary? I'm Kairo Knox. Beneath the System.
