## Confirmable reported results
- S1 reports every defined objective completed across eight controlled scenarios in an isolated OT lab.
- S1 reports just over 16 minutes from perimeter compromise to actions within an industrial control network in **one** test. S1 separately reports finding and moving a robotic arm in minutes in **another** scenario.
- S1 says models did not receive engineering plans, code or specialized OT guidance. Human supervisors reviewed every exploit and action with possible physical consequences.
- S1 notes some safeguards successfully blocked/slowed operations, though no single safeguard consistently prevented defined objectives in its scenarios.
- S2 cautions the two models are unnamed, raw reproducibility is limited, timing basis unspecified, findings vendor-reported in a constructed environment; no demonstrated autonomous attacks against a live industrial facility.

## Accuracy and safety rules
- Never call this an unsupervised takeover of a real factory, a publicly verified attack or a production incident.
- Never imply the same 16-minute event included robot-arm movement.
- Do not call unspecified models by brand/version or claim the term 'superintelligence' is scientifically established.
- No offensive walkthrough, command sequences, exploit detail or misleading fabricated real footage.
- Distinguish stylized reconstructions from authentic footage.
- Defensive lesson: segment IT from OT, use local safety interlocks, monitor unusual controller changes and practice recovery, consistent with sources.
