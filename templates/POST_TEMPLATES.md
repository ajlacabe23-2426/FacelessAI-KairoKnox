# Kairo Knox — Reusable Publishing Templates

Use these as **adaptable structures**, not identical posts reused across every platform. Start with the eight-step [Creative Director guide](../docs/CREATIVE_DIRECTOR.md); leave unsupported research/analytics fields as N/A.

**Change:** We worked on [public-safe feature, component or workflow].

**Still open:** [One honest limitation, open test or release gate].

**Why it matters:** [Customer/security/user consequence in ordinary language].

**Question:** Which would you prioritize next: [meaningful option A] or [meaningful option B]—and why?

## Threads — Kairo Debate Drop (text-first; 1–3 short sentences)

## Instagram carousel — seven-slide default
1. **Cover:** [Original concrete hook, normally fewer than 10 words.]
2. **Why now:** [One context point.]
3. **The problem:** [One consequence or misconception.]
4. **The proof:** [One source-backed demonstration, chart or example.]
5. **The mechanism:** [One clear cause, comparison or relevant constraint.]
6. **Save this:** [One actionable checklist/diagram/framework.]
7. **Your view:** [One specific, answerable question.]
Keep each slide's *primary text* generally under 20 words, one primary idea per slide; provide source citations and accessible details separately as necessary. Rewrite the two weakest slides for genuine usefulness. Avoid cliffhangers with no payoff.
